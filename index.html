📖 How to get your free RapidAPI key

1
Open the API page

Go to rapidapi.com/ytjar/api/yt-api — this is the YT-API by ytjar used by this app.

2
Sign up / Log in to RapidAPI

Create a free account (Google sign-in works) or log in if you already have one.

3
Subscribe to the free plan

Click the blue Subscribe to Test button on that page, then choose the BASIC plan (it's free, no credit card needed).

4
Copy your API key

After subscribing, go to the Endpoints tab. On the right panel under Header Parameters, find X-RapidAPI-Key. Click the copy icon next to that value — that's your key.

5
Paste below and save

Paste the key into the field below and click Save Settings. You're all set!
